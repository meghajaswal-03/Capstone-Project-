# Capstone-Project-
capstone project ideas in progress 

Overall topic 1 : Mental health support 

Idea 1: Between sessions 
Problem: People in therapy usually see their once a week or less and sometimes only once a month, depending on their situation. In between sessions, there is little to no support. Over the days, they forget what they felt, what came up, what they wanted to talk about or even help needed at the moment. 
Solution: A simple space for daily check-ins(mood, energy, a short note), journaling prompts, and a list of topics to bring to the next appointment. before a session, the app could summarizes recent entries so the user can walk in prepared. It is support tool, not a replacement for therapy, and it includes easy access to crisis resources. 
Data: Mostly user-generated: check-ins, journal entries, and saved topics, plus a library of journaling prompts. 

Idea 2: Mood and Environment Tracker 
Problem: Many people feel like their mood changes with the weather, their sleep, or how much daylight they get, but they can't see the pattern. Without data, it just a guess. 
Solution: Users log their mood each day, and the app adds outside information like weather and sunrise and sunset times. Charts show how the two line up over weeks and months. 
Data: The user's daily mood logs, plus public weather and daylight data from a outside source. 

Topic 2: Job Search 
Problem: Job seekers keep track of applications in spreadsheets, notes, and email threads .It's easy to lose track of where each application stands, who they talked to, and when to follow up. 
Solution: One board that shows each application's stage (applied, screening, interviewing, offer, closed), with saved contacts,  company notes, follow-up reminders, and a record of which resume version went to which role. 
Data: The user's own application records, plus real job posting information from a public listing source.
